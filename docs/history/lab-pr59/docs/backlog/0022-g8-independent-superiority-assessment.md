# 0022 — G8: independent superiority assessment

- **Status:** open, priority C1. [PRD-0010](../prd/0010-powercoder-physics-grounded-engineering-agent.md) G8. Depends on G3, G6 and G7.

## Scope
- **RestoreBench at full scale:** both corpora; S0081 is excluded or footnoted per
  [PowerAgentBench#13](https://github.com/Power-Agent/PowerAgentBench/issues/13).
- **PowerAgentBench Levels 1–2:** only once licensed
  ([#11](https://github.com/Power-Agent/PowerAgentBench/issues/11)).
- **Dynamic benchmarks:** where tooling and licences allow.
- **Forecasting:** matched against authoritative forecasts.
- **Engineering investigations:** blind tasks.

## Human protocol
- **Comparable conditions:** the same task, evidence access and time limits.
- **Blind scoring:** blind to authorship, with criteria fixed before any result is collected.
- **Separate arms:** unaided engineers, tool-assisted engineers and the agent are distinguished.

## Exit criteria
- Superiority hypotheses and thresholds are preregistered.
- Results are reproducible, with uncertainty reported.
- Safety and validity requirements are met.
- One rubric is used throughout.
- Unsupported claims are excluded.

No gate authorises autonomous operational control (PC-CON-002/003).
