Tables (AEMO MMS data via NEMOSIS; every table covers the 2025-09-01 market day: SETTLEMENTDATE runs from 2025-09-01 00:05:00 to 2025-09-02 00:00:00 inclusive, all rows are the data window, so do not filter by calendar date; always use INTERVENTION = 0):
- dispatchload(SETTLEMENTDATE TIMESTAMP, DUID VARCHAR, INTERVENTION INT, TOTALCLEARED DOUBLE, AVAILABILITY DOUBLE, ...): one row per dispatch unit per 5-minute interval; TOTALCLEARED is the unit's dispatched MW. Energy in MWh = MW * 5 / 60.
- dudetailsummary(DUID, START_DATE, END_DATE, REGIONID, STATIONID, PARTICIPANTID, DISPATCHTYPE, ...): maps each DUID to its region and station. A DUID can have SEVERAL rows (different START_DATE); the row in force is the one with the LATEST START_DATE for that DUID. Use only that row when joining.
- dispatchprice(SETTLEMENTDATE, REGIONID, INTERVENTION, RRP DOUBLE, ...): regional price in $/MWh, one row per region per interval.
- dispatchregionsum(SETTLEMENTDATE, REGIONID, INTERVENTION, TOTALDEMAND DOUBLE, NETINTERCHANGE DOUBLE, ...): regional demand in MW; NETINTERCHANGE positive means the region is exporting.
- dispatchinterconnectorres(SETTLEMENTDATE, INTERCONNECTORID, INTERVENTION, MWFLOW DOUBLE, MWLOSSES DOUBLE, ...): flow in MW per interconnector per interval; positive MWFLOW is the interconnector's forward direction (VIC1-NSW1 forward means from VIC1 to NSW1).
Regions: NSW1, QLD1, SA1, TAS1, VIC1. SETTLEMENTDATE is the END of the 5-minute interval.
